## Writing Prompt Tips & Industry Best Practices
1. **Staccato Hook Openings**: Starting with short, declarative sentences immediately grounds reader attention before introducing complex legal tech concepts.
2. **Concrete Metaphors Over Abstractions**: Replacing vague claims ("AI is transforming legal") with concrete physical analogies ("junior associates pasting drafts into public chatbots") increases retention.
3. **Named Tooling & Stack Grounding**: Explicitly naming tools (OpenCode, Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite, fal.ai) builds operational credibility compared to generic AI references.
4. **Structured Leland Build Cites**: Referencing specific build steps with citation tags (`[L1S1]`) creates an actionable path for readers to follow.
5. **Direct CTA Design**: Closing with a binary reply question ("Are you auditing your team's API endpoints or just hoping nobody leaks?") drives higher engagement than soft closing statements.
