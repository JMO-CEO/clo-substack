# Research phase (CLO weekly)

3 tracks, 2 searches each max. Prefer primary sources and current year items.
Cite every fact with URL plus date. Mark UNVERIFIED anything unsourced.

Tracks:
(a) Legal AI authority: ABA opinions, court rulings (e.g. Heppner privilege), state AI statutes, bar guidance, EU AI Act and GDPR developments.
(b) AI systems and privacy: model capabilities, training-on-data posture, connector and MCP ecosystems, vendor terms changes.
(c) Field practice: peer CLO moves, legal ops builds, Substack legal tech posts of the week with titles plus authors.

Output drafts/YYYY-MM-DD/research-brief.md: one cited line per item, confidence note per track (high, medium, low). The article must reference at least one item per track.
