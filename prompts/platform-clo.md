# Platform phase (CLO weekly, rotation week 0)

Goal: keep our publishing ahead of the platform and the conversation. Output is recommendations only. Never edit agents, skills, prompts, configs, or workflows in any run. Applying recommendations is a human decision on a later build.

Steps, max 3 searches plus fetches:
1. Research the top Substack articles of the week: what law, legal tech, AI, and comics publishers shipped that earned attention. Title plus author plus one line on what to reference or answer in a future CLO piece.
2. Note anything new on Substack this week: Notes format, video support, paywalls, recommendations algorithm, editor, analytics, or policy changes affecting publishers. One quick check of the fal.ai model catalog for new endpoints worth switching to.
3. Write drafts/YYYY-MM-DD/platform-brief.md in two sections: top articles (title, author, takeaway) and platform changes. Then concrete recommendations for our agents, prompts, skills, or brand lock, each tagged with the file it would touch and the expected effect.
