# Article phase (CLO weekly)

Load clo-persuasion-coach for structure, clo-leland-ops when showing a build, clo-humanize-writing for the AI-tell strip, clo-humanize-voice for the final pass.

Shape: Hook to Story to Insight to Solution to CTA. 1000 to 1600 words.
First 3 lines carry the hook. Title under 70 chars, subtitle under 140.
Name the real stack, never vague AI tools. One Leland cite [LxSy] per build shown.
Close: Next Week teaser (one line) plus For now reply question. Optional reply keyword in caps.
Metadata block: title options, subtitle, tags, SEO slug, 2 pull quotes.
After drafting, list specifics still needed, never fabricate.
No hedging, no em dashes, no emojis. Journal judgment only, never legal advice.
