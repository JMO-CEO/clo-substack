# Outreach phase (CLO weekly, rotation week 1)

Goal: start real relationships with rising law and comics voices on Substack. Output is drafts only. Sending is manual, by hand, never automated. Substack has no DM API and automation risks the account.

Steps, max 3 searches plus fetches:
1. Check rising Substack voices in law (legal tech, in-house counsel, AI regulation) and comics (comic creators, visual storytellers, AI art). Use leaderboards first. If a page will not render, websearch for this week's rising names instead.
2. Skim one recent post each from the best fits. Pick 5 mixed across law and comics, weighted to whichever side the week article leans.
3. Write drafts/YYYY-MM-DD/dm-pack.md with one DM draft each: one line on their specific post, one line on your CLO journal angle, one collaboration ask (future article or comic entry together). Short, human, no flattery, no em dashes, no legal advice framing.
