## Core Positioning

**Blend of:** AI Governance as Competitive Advantage (#2) + In-House Counsel Perspective on Legal Innovation (#3)

Professional judgment is the balance point between velocity and excellence.

Use AI to say yes quickly, but with guardrails so AI doesn't make mistakes. Governance isn't a compliance checkbox—it's how you enable both speed and certainty.

### What This Means (Operationally)

- **Proof:** Show what we build at Estate Guru to demonstrate this works.

- **Secondary:** Legal ops leaders, compliance teams, in-house technologists thinking about how to scale legal.

2. **I run Estate Guru's legal operations.** Estate planning is compliance-heavy, UPL-constrained, and high-stakes. If you can do AI governance here, you can do it anywhere.

## Writing North Star (Per Underline Principles)

**Judgment:** I lead with decisiveness, not hedging. "Here's what we learned. Here's what I'd do." Complete information isn't the goal—clear thinking is.

**Velocity:** I respect the reader's time. Direct, practical, scannable. No theory without application.

**Bedside Manner:** "Inquiry ⇒ Judgment ⇒ Simplification ⇒ Delivery." I explain what matters without dumbing it down. Plain language. No legalese.

**Winning:** I'm writing to help other CLOs win. If my angle helps you build a better legal operations, I win too. No gatekeeping.

---

## Tone Rules

- **Direct, not defensive.** State what happened. State what it means. No hedges like "arguably" or "some might say."
- **Grounded, not theoretical.** Every point is tied to something real: a problem we solved, a mistake we made, a pattern we saw.
- **Confident, not arrogant.** "Here's what worked for us" not "here's what everyone should do."
- **Practical, not perfect.** I show trade-offs. I show what failed. I show the iteration.
- **Human, not institutional.** "I built this" not "the team built this." Take credit and responsibility.
